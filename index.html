🧠 RISK PSYCHOLOGY — KNOW THIS
◆ Risk 1% per trade max when starting. At 1%, you can lose 20 in a row and still have 82% left. At 5%, 20 losses = 36% left. The math is unforgiving.
◆ Set your stop loss first. Lot size is a calculation, not a decision. Never enter a trade and figure out risk afterward.
◆ After 3 consecutive losses, drop to 0.5% for the next trade. Losing streaks are real. Protect the account, not your ego.
◆ A 1:2 RR at 50% win rate is profitable. You don't need to be right 70% of the time — cut losses fast and let winners run.
◆ Daily loss limit = 3% of account max. Hit it → close the platform. No revenge trades. No recovery attempts. Tomorrow is a new session.
◆ Increasing lot size after a loss to "make it back" is how accounts blow up. Same size, every trade, every day.
